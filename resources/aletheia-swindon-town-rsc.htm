Heritage • Hotel • Weddings

Lydiard Park

Lydiard combines Grade I listed Lydiard House, 260 acres of parkland, St Mary's Church, an 18th-century Walled Garden, hotel accommodation, weddings, conferences and events.

The Walled Garden was created by the St John family in the 1740s and reopened after restoration in 2007.

HISTORIC ENGLAND

Lost Swindon corner

Luck Skywalkers / Skywalkers Promotions

Historic directories record a fancy-dress business called Luck Skywalkers at 33 Faringdon Road and Skywalkers Promotions in the Brunel Centre. Treat it as historical, not a current shop.

The surviving Spider-Man sign is retained here as local recollection rather than independently verified historical evidence.
