Lydiard Park, House, Hotel & Events

Official site:

https://www.lydiardpark.org.uk/

Lydiard Park deserves a fuller resource entry than simply "parkland".

The estate currently combines:

  • Lydiard House Museum, a historic country house associated with the St John family for more than 500 years;
  • 260 acres of parkland;
  • St Mary's Church, with important St John family monuments;
  • the restored 18th-century Walled Garden;
  • Lydiard Park Hotel & Conference Centre, with guest accommodation;
  • weddings and civil ceremonies;
  • receptions and private celebrations;
  • conferences and corporate events;
  • seasonal events, outdoor cinema, live music, heritage events and family activities;
  • café / tea-room facilities.

Weddings and hotel

Lydiard Park Hotel forms part of the wider historic estate.

Wedding and event information:

https://www.lydiardpark.org.uk/weddings/

Hotel / conferencing:

https://www.lydiardpark.org.uk/hotel/conferencing/

The Walled Garden

The Walled Garden is a restored 18th-century ornamental fruit and flower garden, created by the St John family in the 1740s.

Walled Garden:

https://www.lydiardpark.org.uk/visit/walled-garden/

History

Useful historical hooks include:

  • the St John family held Lydiard for more than 500 years;
  • Margaret Beauchamp, through whom the estate passed to the St Johns, was grandmother to Henry VII;
  • the estate contains medieval, Tudor, Civil War and Georgian history;
  • during the Second World War the grounds contained an American station hospital associated with the 101st Airborne Division and treated D-Day casualties;
  • it later became a hospital for German prisoners of war;
  • the GWR Home Guard also trained in the park;
  • Swindon Corporation bought the estate in 1943.

History:

https://www.lydiardpark.org.uk/visit/museum/history/

1. Existing affiliate link

  • books;
  • customised gifts;
  • generic merchandise.

  • NO_AFFILIATE — useful link with no monetisation
  • UNKNOWN — needs checking
  • HISTORICAL — no longer a current commercial resource
