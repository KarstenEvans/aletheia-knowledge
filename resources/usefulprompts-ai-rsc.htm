15 handy AI instruction tools from UsefulPrompts.ai

A free external collection for turning rough ideas into usable instructions. Here is what each tool is for and where a similar original workflow might belong within Aletheia.

Original creator and site: UsefulPrompts.ai (creator posts as @usefulpromptsai on Threads). A user-provided 12 October 2026 Threads screenshot showed a five-step short-video workflow attributed to this creator. Their live site has 15 generators; the five-step screenshot is a separate social-media example, not proof that the site ships a full five-step video editor.

How this research should be used: The underlying idea of task-specific prompt templates is reusable, but this page is an original catalogue, not a mirror or endorsement. Do not reproduce UsefulPrompts' distinctive prose, cards or visual assets. AI-independent Markdown and an existing Aletheia app/Skill should be preferred over another near-duplicate app.

A five-stage short-video lesson

The creator's Threads post illustrated: draft a brief script, plan each camera shot, work with existing equipment, write sound-off captions, and optionally compose a concise description for an AI-video generator.

Aletheia's own lesson adds privacy and source checks, realistic physical camera staging, a teach-first worked example, exportable scene/caption tables, and a distinction between clean footage and later captions. Open Aletheia Learn (select Try the video lesson).
