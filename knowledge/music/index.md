# Aletheia Knowledge — Music

**Status:** Active knowledge shelf  
**Started:** 2026-09-24  
**Canonical folder:** `knowledge/music/`

Aletheia Music studies the small pieces of language that escape songs and live in memory: titles, hooks, short lyric phrases, questions, commands and compact ideas.

This is not a lyrics archive. Keep lyric quotation short, link to legitimate lyric/song pages, and concentrate Aletheia's original work on **why wording sticks, what emotional vibe it carries, and where the idea leads when we go walkabout**.

## Catchphrase classes

**CORE CATCHPHRASE** — memorable wording from a song that reached roughly the UK Top 10 or another major-market Top 10, or another clearly documented major-hit threshold.

**ARTIST PHRASE** — wording strong enough to preserve in an artist's knowledge even when the single itself misses the Core chart rule.

Chart performance and phrase quality are separate facts. Do not bend one to fit the other.
