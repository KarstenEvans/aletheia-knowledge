🎵 Aletheia Music

Song catchphrases, artist knowledge and the little pieces of language that escape a song and live in people's heads.

THE IDEA

Start with a phrase such as “Nothing ever lasts forever”, then walk outward: song → artist → songwriter → emotion → language trick → culture → history → philosophy → related music → useful resources.

This is not a lyrics archive. Aletheia keeps lyric quotation short and concentrates on original analysis, sources and connections.
