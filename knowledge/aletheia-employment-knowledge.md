## JOB-001 | What is an ATS?
### Summary
An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is recruitment software used to manage and sometimes parse or screen applications. Tailor your CV to a **particular** advert with truthful relevant language and simple headings. There is no universal passing score.
### Practice
Use one-column formatting where practical, readable fonts, consistent dates, Work Experience / Skills / Education headings; avoid important information in pictures, tables and decorative objects; use the employer's requested format. Maintain the original CV and a vacancy-specific copy.
### Sources
- UK Government JobHelp ATS: https://jobhelp.campaign.gov.uk/improve-your-chances-of-getting-a-job/cv-job-applications-interviews/make-your-cv-stand-out/getting-through-application-filters-applicant-tracking-systems/ (checked 2026-10-02)
- National Careers Service CV: https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections

## JOB-002 | CV vs job advert: evidence matrix
### Summary
Compare **essential** and **desirable** employer requirements with skills and achievements the person can genuinely document. A missing statement in the CV does not necessarily mean the person lacks that skill.
### Practice
Make three columns: job requirement, exact CV supporting evidence, honest revision/questions. Put responsibilities into meaningful outcomes without making up figures. Ask for missing information before rewriting.
### Sources
- National Careers Service: https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections

## JOB-003 | Live job versus ghost vacancy
### Summary
An individual vacancy may be labelled apparently open only after inspecting its employer or authorised recruitment application page at a recorded date. Aggregated snippets, general company careers pages and historic adverts are leads rather than proof.
### Practice
Capture job title, employer, location, job ID where visible, direct application URL, checked timestamp, actual accept-applications signal and known uncertainties. Duplicate role ads should be grouped and reported as duplicates when evidence supports it.
### Sources
- Official UK discovery: https://www.gov.uk/find-a-job
- Method: Aletheia Job Search canonical Markdown (author-defined evidence contract, not an external fraud statistic).

## JOB-004 | Recruitment fraud checks
### Summary
Verify the organisation, actual recruiting contact, careers domain, application path, payment requests and suspicious pressure. Warning signs call for verification, not automatic accusations about a company.
### Practice
Cross-check official employer website and relevant registration authorities. Be cautious about bank details or identity documents before a legitimate employer process is established, and requests to pay for placement/training to release an alleged job.
### Sources
- Aletheia Trust Check method, user-provided source (framework rather than a fraud finding).

## JOB-005 | LinkedIn profile improvement
### Summary
Keep the headline, About, roles, skills and dates factually consistent with the CV. Use recognisable professional terms that reflect actual work, not lists stuffed with unearned keywords.
### Practice
Offer an amended headline and an evidence-led About paragraph. Review discoverability and privacy choices with the user. Do not infer access to private LinkedIn data.
### Sources
- LinkedIn official help: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin

## JOB-006 | Interview practice and alternative routes
### Summary
Use STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) with concrete real examples. Practise a question at a time, ask for feedback and verify employer-specific facts. If jobs are scarce, a bounded Go Walkabout can identify adjacent roles without pretending they are vacancies.
### Practice
Prepare three real STAR examples and three questions for the employer. For a Walkabout test, try transferable skills, overlooked employers and adjacent job titles, verify any advertised opportunity, then stop.
### Sources
- National Careers Service: https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/interview-advice/the-star-method
