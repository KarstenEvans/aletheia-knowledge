## Direct answer

The reusable idea is **one editorial system rather than twelve disconnected prompts**: a central idea inbox, sources, goals, topic pillars, a truthful author voice, intended audience, drafts/assets, review, status, publication record and real performance evidence. Chris markets a paid Notion template and four bonus mini-courses around those concepts. Aletheia can implement its **own** smaller, portable Markdown-first workflow without copying proprietary Notion templates, course materials, testimonials or artwork, buying the product, requiring Notion, or granting AI automatic publishing authority.

The supplied sales page is useful evidence of **what the product seller advertises**. It is not evidence that buyers save 100+ hours per month, that following the framework produces millions of followers, or that every testimonial and outcome has been independently verified.

### What not to infer
- “No subscriptions” does not establish that **Notion**, every integration or social-scheduler upgrade is free for all uses; assess implementation needs independently.
- A unified planning calendar is **not** proof of direct posting to LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. Publishing connectors, authorisation, platform terms and availability are distinct.
- An “avatar” or persona is a writing aid, not permission to invent a follower's identity, traits, pain or real testimonial.
- No follower-growth, lead-generation, automatic posting, course-completion or return-on-investment outcome has been demonstrated for an Aletheia implementation.

## AK-COS-001 | One source of truth for ideas

### Summary
Capture inspiration in a central inbox with the original URL and a brief note about the useful question it raises. Don't copy another creator's work wholesale. Separate collected inspiration from your own original observation.

**Type:** ALETHEIA EDITORIAL PATTERN · **Evidence:** CREATOR OS ADVERTISED FEATURE + INDEPENDENT DESIGN · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Reusable method
Fields: idea ID, date, one-line question, origin/source URL, original creator, topic pillar, target reader, possible useful result, evidence to find, overlap/duplicate key, and status. Accept “no actionable idea” as a legitimate outcome.

### For Aletheia
An item found in LinkedIn, an Instagram reel or a saved WhatsApp link enters the **ideas inbox**, not a public-page queue. Review for source permissions and a genuinely original angle. Link existing Aletheia Knowledge/Swindon.org.uk material rather than spawning thin duplicates.

## AK-COS-002 | Content pillars and an honest author voice

### Summary
Define a small number of subjects a person is qualified to discuss and a voice that sounds like their real writing, not a generated personality pretending to have experiences.

**Type:** ALETHEIA EDITORIAL PATTERN · **Evidence:** CREATOR OS ADVERTISED FOUNDATIONS PAGE + ALETHEIA DESIGN · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Reusable method
A lightweight voice card: audience, subject pillars, practical knowledge, preferred level of detail, tone, prohibited claims, real firsthand examples, source requirements and how humour is used. Examples of possible Aletheia lanes: source-checking, useful software tools, CAD/BIM workflows, original local research, and creative projects. Each post needs **one main purpose**.

### Guardrails
Never fabricate customers, project outcomes, first-person anecdotes, authority, review counts or quoted endorsements. AI may draft and suggest alternatives, but the owner edits, approves and remains responsible for the published content. LinkedIn itself asks AI-assisted material to reflect real human perspective.

- https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1481496
- https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol

## AK-COS-003 | Audience questions without invented personas

### Summary
Turn actual audience questions into a useful explanation. “Follower avatars” are a prompt to understand a real readership, not verified demographic or psychological profiling.

**Type:** ALETHEIA CONTENT DESIGN · **Evidence:** ADVERTISED CREATOR OS PERSONA FEATURE + DESIGN INFERENCE · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Reusable method
Record one intended reader group, a real question they might ask, terminology used in publicly available sources or voluntarily supplied feedback, what they need to accomplish, and what is unknown. Separate an illustrative hypothetical from a claim about real people. Avoid sensitive-attribute inference or personal dossiers.

### Example
A MicroStation coordinator asks, “Which of fifty read-only iDGN references produces the startup warning?” Aletheia can write a source-traced diagnostic development note, without pretending to have measured a production result.

### Sources
- User-supplied Creator OS text (the “Know exactly who you're speaking to” offer).

## AK-COS-004 | A publication state machine, not a blind posting queue

### Summary
A single item progresses through explicit and reversible editorial states. Merely reaching a scheduled date must never confer permission to post.

**Type:** ALETHEIA WORKFLOW DESIGN · **Evidence:** ADVERTISED UNIFIED CALENDAR + INDEPENDENT SPECIFICATION · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Suggested states
`CAPTURED → RESEARCHING → DRAFT → FACT-CHECKED → NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW → APPROVED → SCHEDULED → PUBLISHED → MEASURED → ARCHIVED`. Alternative exits: `DUPLICATE`, `NO POST`, `BLOCKED`, `REJECTED`, `EXPIRED`, `CORRECTION REQUIRED`.

Keep `approved_by`, approval date and the exact approved content revision, destination, platform-specific copy, source receipts and any actual resulting post URL. Corrections return to human review. One approval should not silently authorise all later edits, platforms or reposts.

### Why this matters
A normal Notion database can have `Status`, `Date`, person, tags and multiple table/board/calendar views; these facilities are confirmed by Notion documentation, but they do not require Aletheia to use Notion or to buy Creator OS.

### Sources
- https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/database-properties
- https://www.notion.com/help/calendars

## AK-COS-005 | One canonical idea, distinct platform editions

### Summary
LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube do not need four unrelated source documents. Keep one research/source record and create optional platform-specific editions only where format and audience justify them.

**Type:** ALETHEIA CROSS-CHANNEL PATTERN · **Evidence:** ADVERTISED MULTIPLATFORM CALENDAR + INDEPENDENT DESIGN · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Reusable method
One parent `content_id` links drafts and approved variants. Separate the short hook, alt text, media permission, description, call to action, link, publication time and resulting URL for each platform. Track whether each platform supports automatic publishing through a permitted authorised route. No assumption of available integrations.

### Guardrails
Respect rights to video, thumbnails, graphics, logos and supplied course text. Preserve the original source's attribution and do not pass off a curated collection as an original claim to those underlying resources.

### Sources
- User-supplied Creator OS advertisement.
- https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1481496

## AK-COS-006 | Idea generation is an evidence-led filter, not a volume target

### Summary
“100+ ideas” is an advertised course title, not a requirement or proof of originality. Collecting a hundred vague hooks has less value than preserving a small number of clear, well-supported questions.

**Type:** ALETHEIA EDITORIAL PATTERN · **Evidence:** ADVERTISED BONUS-COURSE DESCRIPTION + INDEPENDENT DESIGN · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Useful prompts for an original item
What surprised us? Which question can we actually answer? What claim needs checking? What did a real project teach? Which source changed an earlier conclusion? Which practical action can readers perform? Has this been covered already? What is missing or uncertain? If none produces substantive information, **NO POST TODAY** is a successful answer.

## AK-COS-007 | Useful post structure and visual design

### Summary
An opening line should identify a concrete question or result; the body supplies evidence and useful explanation; visuals should clarify the subject rather than promise to “convert”.

**Type:** ALETHEIA WRITING/ACCESSIBILITY PATTERN · **Evidence:** ADVERTISED WRITING/DESIGN MINI-COURSES + INDEPENDENT DESIGN · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Original reusable outline
`REAL QUESTION → DIRECT ANSWER → HOW WE KNOW → PRACTICAL STEPS / EXAMPLE → LIMITATION → RELEVANT SOURCE / LINK → OPTIONAL DISCUSSION QUESTION`.

For a visual: choose one message; legible mobile type, sufficient contrast, concise caption, alt text, appropriate rights and original evidence. Source dates and unresolved facts survive any graphical simplification. Avoid untested formula promises, generic AI artwork and invented personal success stories.

## AK-COS-008 | Collaboration and provenance can be simple

### Summary
Even one author benefits from recording who proposed, checked, edited, approved and published a change. A dashboard only has value if it makes those decisions easier.

**Type:** ALETHEIA WORKFLOW DESIGN · **Evidence:** ADVERTISED COLLABORATION FEATURE + ALETHEIA PROTOCOL · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Minimal record
`content_id, owner, editor, checker, approval_id, latest_revision, due_date, open_questions, source_receipts, rights_status, final_url`. Roles may all belong to one person. Don't construct a role-heavy service merely to mimic a paid template. Keep GitHub as the portable canonical master, and treat Notion or a future Aletheia UI as an optional view.

## AK-COS-009 | Measure usefulness, not follower-count mythology

### Summary
Measure observable results on real, dated posts rather than treating seller testimonials, 3-million-follower claims or a “100 hours saved” headline as performance evidence for the proposed Aletheia workflow.

**Type:** ALETHEIA MEASUREMENT PATTERN · **Evidence:** UNVERIFIED CREATOR OS PROMOTION + INDEPENDENT EXPERIMENT DESIGN · **Checked:** 2026-09-29

### Proposed measures
Time spent from research to approved content, errors corrected before publication, duplicates avoided, real inquiries/questions, actual post URLs, observed engagement in context, whether readers completed a useful linked action and explicitly recorded source corrections. Record comparison period, platform, population and limitations. Do not derive a causal improvement from a before/after anecdote alone.

## Proposed Aletheia architecture: extend the existing Publisher, do not clone Creator OS

Keep the existing **Aletheia LinkedIn Check / proposed Publisher** as owner of research-led drafting, voice card, review, duplicate detection and optional lawful publication/export. A portable `content/` shelf (future work, not created now) could contain:

```text
content/
  ideas.md                   # short original leads with provenance
  editorial-profile.md       # genuine author voice, themes and audience
  content-register.csv       # stable IDs and workflow status
  posts/<content-id>.md      # canonical source receipts and drafts
  platforms/<content-id>-linkedin.md  # optional reviewed edition
  reviews/<content-id>.md    # human acceptance and correction receipts
```

**Illustrative record, not an installed schema:**

```yaml
content_id: AL-PUB-0001
origin: https://example.org/source
origin_creator: named original source
pillar: source-checking
real_question: What will this reader learn or do?
intended_reader: clearly described without personal profiling
source_check: NEEDS_RESEARCH
rights: REVIEW
status: DRAFT
human_approval: NOT_GRANTED
approved_revision: null
platforms: [linkedin]
scheduled_for: null
published_url: null
result: UNMEASURED
```

**Decision gate:** publish only when there is a real point, a checked source, permission for any media, a recognisable human voice and explicit approval. Platform automation is optional and must conform to its terms. No recurring subscription, Notion template copy, automatic outreach or API setup follows merely from this research note.
