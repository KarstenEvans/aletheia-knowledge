[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) · [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md)

Original collection: Chris Donnelly's LinkedIn graphic and post, https://lnkd.in/p/e9PRGswQ (two supplied screenshots, 29 September 2026). Aletheia wrote these concise knowledge cards and exercises independently; this is neither a copy of the original courses nor an endorsement from their publishers.

**Corrective context:** The graphic says "12 free AI guides" and mentions certificates. These are mixed material types, not twelve independently established qualifications. The three Searchable resources are from a business the post author co-founded; entries 10–12 link to his own promotion. The first-party Skilljar pages were searchable in official site indexes but resisted direct fetch; the Skills PDF, Searchable guides and OpenAI Academy landing pages were accessible in web retrieval. The last three links are screenshot transcriptions only. Access, signup, price, licensing and plan availability can change.

**Useful Aletheia method:** Separate *what the original source states*, *what a directly checked official document establishes*, *what Aletheia infers or proposes as a portable workflow*, and *what is unavailable for verification*. Never treat advertising statistics, completion badges or listed links as proof of efficacy.

## AI-12-13 | Reusable AI Skills are knowledge plus tests, not magical prompts

### Summary
A repeatable AI Skill should have a narrow trigger, explicit inputs and outputs, a stable method, clear permissions, and an observable acceptance test. A tool connection supplies access, while a Skill supplies reusable working instructions.

**Type:** ALETHEIA WORKFLOW / OFFICIAL ANTHROPIC GUIDE  
**Evidence:** PRIMARY-PDF + INDEPENDENT ALETHEIA DESIGN  
**Last checked:** 2026-09-29

### Details
The Anthropic guide describes SKILL.md and optional scripts, references and assets. For portable Aletheia practice, keep the same architecture principle as a provider-independent Markdown procedure with: goal, trigger, scope, source receipts, exclusions, handoff, acceptance and a failure pathway. Aletheia Protocol preserves evidence and correction, while Thalia may add optional tone. Validate with a known-positive, known-negative and ambiguous test before wider rollout. This general pattern may inform future iDGN diagnostic developer instructions, but is not a claim of MicroStation SDK compatibility.

## AI-12-14 | Answer-first writing without AEO superstition

### Summary
A readable question-and-answer page has an actual question, a concise useful answer, detailed context, source receipts, an honest reviewed date and links to related work. This helps humans first and can make an answer easier for search and retrieval systems to parse.

**Type:** ALETHEIA EDITORIAL PATTERN / VENDOR-SUGGESTED  
**Evidence:** SEARCHABLE VENDOR CHECKLIST + INDEPENDENT CONTENT DESIGN  
**Last checked:** 2026-09-29

### Details
An example for Swindon.org.uk: state the answer in ordinary static HTML ahead of dynamic widgets, then provide practical steps, limits and independent source links. Do not mass-publish near-duplicate keyword pages. Do not assert that schema, any particular answer length, changing robots directives or `llms.txt` guarantees indexing, AI citation or traffic. Separate an experiment's impressions/clicks from any self-reported vendor conversion claim. Record date, query, observed answer, actual citation URL and retrieval conditions when monitoring.

### Sources
- https://www.searchable.com/blog/what-is-aeo
- https://www.searchable.com/content-playbook
- https://www.searchable.com/claude-visibility-report

## AI-12-15 | Free training, paid functions and marketing lead magnets are different things

### Summary
Classify an educational link by provider, actual resource type, account requirement, certificate type (if any), product entitlement and commercial relationship. "Free registration" for a learning page does not promise free entitlement to associated software; a lead-magnet download can collect contact details.

**Type:** ALETHEIA SOURCE-TRUST CLASSIFICATION  
**Evidence:** PRIMARY COURSE INDEXES + ORIGINAL PROMOTIONAL SCREENSHOT  
**Last checked:** 2026-09-29

### Details
For twelve-guide collections, separate official training, publicly readable PDF, vendor blog/playbook/report, and creator promotion. Record inaccessible URLs as `NOT INDEPENDENTLY OPENED`, not `SCAM`, `FREE` or `DEAD`. The screenshot is evidence of what a post advertised, not of actual resource contents or downstream terms. Encourage readers to check permissions and privacy policies rather than submit passwords or sensitive client information.
