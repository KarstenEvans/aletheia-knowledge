## Supplied sources reviewed in the September 2026 pass

### Directly useful for AI Knowledge

#### AI Fundamentals — The Cheat Sheet
Publisher/context: Property Filter, 2026.

Useful discovery themes:
- AI outcome depends on the combination of model, interface, tools and context;
- permissions should distinguish reading, drafting and sending/acting;
- repeatable workflows can be captured as Skills;
- treating an AI as a worker requires a real brief, tools and context rather than only Q&A.

Aletheia use:
- capability-stack card;
- action-permission ladder;
- Skill-versus-app rules.

#### AI Adoption For Dummies — WalkMe Special Edition
Publisher: Wiley / WalkMe, 2026.

Useful discovery themes:
- enterprise AI adoption depends on workflow context and cross-application execution;
- assistants/agents are incomplete without access to the real work context;
- guidance should appear where work happens;
- adoption should be governed as a workflow/change problem, not merely a model purchase.

Aletheia use:
- workflow integration before agent proliferation;
- context/tool/permission design;
- app interfaces that reduce copy/paste friction.

#### The State of Digital Adoption 2026 — WalkMe
Source: [original 42-page report](https://www.walkme.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Soda-Report-2026.pdf), supplied and directly reviewed **2026-10-03**. **WalkMe is an enterprise digital-adoption vendor**, not independent proof of its proposed product's impact.
Full independent extraction: [The AI Execution Gap: research cards and ADOPT audit](aletheia-digital-adoption-execution-gap-2026.md) (**knowledge-only**).

Useful discovery themes:
- execution friction from app switching;
- lost workflow context;
- insufficient in-flow guidance;
- low trust and manual workarounds;
- shadow AI as a symptom of approved tools failing the work.

New evidence boundaries from the full PDF:
- Q1 2026 survey: 1,700 leaders and 2,050 workers across larger organisations; separate behavioural data from WalkMe customers.
- Reported 37% avoided AI due to broken workflows (p. 12); reported 12% fully confident AI knew their work context (p. 22).
- Surveyed workers with in-flow support reported higher training relevance (p. 33); do **not** treat the association as causal.
- 51 lost days/year and $142m/year are vendor estimates/extrapolations, not general measured costs or Aletheia savings.
- Avoid treating one vendor study as representative of small businesses, UK users or all industries.

Aletheia use:
- keep project context inspectable;
- design adapters around real workflows;
- test ADOPT-01..08 and use the existing Hint Ladder at the point of difficulty;
- do not equate more tools/agents with more productivity or copy vendor marketing into product guarantees.

#### The IT Guide — Becoming Frontier with Microsoft 365 Copilot and Agents
Publisher: Microsoft, 2026.

Useful discovery themes:
- staged progression from assistant use to specialised solutions and agentic workflows;
- governance, measurement and skills should accompany automation;
- agentic workflows are better introduced after the underlying process and data sources are understood.

Aletheia use:
- staged capability ladder;
- prove a workflow before automating it;
- separate assistant, specialist workflow and agent execution.

#### Governing AI in 2026
Publisher: OneTrust, 2026.

Useful discovery themes:
- inventory where AI affects decisions;
- risk/rights assessment;
- transparency;
- role/accountability assignment;
- documentation/logging;
- post-deployment monitoring and incident handling.

Aletheia use:
- action receipts;
- explicit operator/authority roles;
- system inventory and review dates;
- evidence of governance rather than policy-only governance.

This is not a substitute for legal advice or checking the actual law in the relevant jurisdiction.

#### The AI Reset
Publisher: Superside, 2026.

Useful discovery themes:

- adoption barriers include skills, workflows, legal/IP concerns and quality;
- select high-impact, lower-risk starting points rather than deploying AI everywhere.

Aletheia use:
- app/Skill promotion test;
- workflow map in page/project specifications;
- pilot before automation.

#### The Ultimate Checklist for Adopting AI at Work
Useful discovery themes:

- set measurable goals;
- consider financial impact and workforce readiness.

Aletheia use:

## UsefulPrompts.ai: task-specific instruction generators and short video workflow (12 October 2026)

**Original creator:** [UsefulPrompts.ai](https://usefulprompts.ai/) and creator's social account **@usefulpromptsai**. **Source checked:** public site on 12 October 2026; user-supplied Threads screenshots of the creator's "5 prompts for short videos with Claude" post supplied the distinct five-stage video example. This is inspiration and link-based credit, not a licence to reproduce the creator's words, graphic cards, or exact proprietary generator implementation.

The site's homepage lists fifteen task-specific generators: Prompt Improver; SEO; LinkedIn; Marketing; YouTube; Etsy; Real Estate; Skill; System; Agent; Resume; Interview; PRD; Image; and Excel Formula. The visible site pattern is one shared instruction-improvement approach and copyable outputs usable in a user-selected AI. Specific tools and features may change. [Original YouTube generator](https://usefulprompts.ai/youtube-prompts).

**Learnable pattern:** Beginner starts with an ordinary-language idea. A task-specific form requests only the missing audience, goal, constraints and output. The app yields a reviewable, portable AI instruction, without insisting on an account or a single AI provider. Do not confuse drafting instructions with carrying out the described work.

**Five-stage video example (separate Threads post):** Create brief script → shot list → plan around equipment already owned → sound-off captions → optional concise AI-video scene description. Original Aletheia lesson [Make a Short Video](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-app/blob/main/aletheia-learn/aletheia-learn-video-making.md) adds teach-first worked examples, real camera staging, privacy, rights, timing and honest footage/render statuses. Clean AI footage and separately edited captions are distinct deliverables.

**Existing-work routing:** video teaching → Aletheia Learn; adapting an existing article → Content Repurposer Skill; SEO/AEO evidence and proposed rewrite → existing GEO/AEO Optimizer Skill and optional Site Audit Stage 6; career prompts → existing Job Search; marketing and LinkedIn → proposed Publisher; PRD/Skill generation → Improve/Dev; stories and image descriptions → Storyteller; other generator ideas → root app `ideas.md` and `tasks.md` until required.

**Status:** Source/discovery register and [credited public resource page](https://karstenevans.github.io/aletheia-knowledge/resources/usefulprompts-ai-rsc.htm). This is not a new AI-provider capability claim, independently functioning tool suite or a copied course; any changing claims require independent verification.
