## Short description

Every AI appears to have disappeared. Yet traffic systems, shops, phones and buildings continue making choices. ToomorrowMan, AI-PI, Schrödinger and the Department of Applied Impossibility investigate Case File 42 and discover why *visibility of a chatbot* is not the same as *visibility of a decision*.

This is a fictional learning story, not a report of a real mass AI disappearance.

## Ten visual chapters

1. **Workshop, storm outside:** the claim that AI has vanished. Show original trio and contrast between AI-PI's presence and the report.
2. **The opened case file:** evidence gathering begins; distinguish eyewitness reports from established facts.
3. **The library:** retrieve evidence, not merely summaries.
4. **Swindon streets:** invisibility of user-facing AI does not equal cessation of background automation.
5. **The machinery:** examine what systems are making decisions and who owns them.
6. **The hall of reflections:** recognise conflicting accounts and false equivalence.
7. **A diffuse AI presence:** foreground decisions, human oversight and provenance, not an invented supernatural explanation.
8. **Everyday objects:** identify where “AI” is a literal model, conventional rule or fictional visual metaphor.
9. **The contained core:** consent, owner, stop conditions and accountability; do not mistake containment for proof of full safety.
10. **Workshop epilogue:** take away the nine questions; end on human agency.

**Editorial reconciliation:** The ten pictures were newly recreated as a thematic set. The existing Case File 42 story has different specific scenes (Elsie, Prudence, quiet room, STOP board). A video editor must align the visual to the actual spoken beat rather than assert that the source text depicts a different action.
