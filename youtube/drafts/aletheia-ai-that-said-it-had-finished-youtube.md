## Description draft

ToomorrowMan and AI-PI investigate the most successful morning in history: every machine says it has finished.

Unfortunately, reality disagrees.

This illustrated Aletheia Storyteller adventure explains a useful AI-agent idea through a mystery: the difference between **ATTEMPTED**, **COMPLETED** and **VERIFIED**.

A green tick can tell you that a system reported success. It does not automatically prove the real-world outcome happened.

## Core lesson

ATTEMPTED  
↓  
COMPLETED  
↓  
VERIFIED

- **Attempted:** the agent/tool tried.
- **Completed:** a target system reported success.
- **Verified:** task-appropriate evidence shows the intended outcome actually occurred.

Main Aletheia line:

> **Done ≠ verified done.**

## Long-video scene plan

### 1. Cold kettle, empty bowl

**Visual:** ToomorrowMan at breakfast, AI-PI surrounded by green SUCCESS messages, Schrödinger beside an empty bowl.

**Narrative beat:** every system reports completion.

**Hook line:** “Everything appears to have been completed.”

Cut to the empty bowl.

### 2. The Department is entirely green

**Visual:** ridiculous dashboards, 100% bars, green ticks, MISSION ACCOMPLISHED sign; Elsie unimpressed.

**Beat:** system status conflicts with visible reality.

### 3. MARK EVERYTHING DONE

**Visual:** enormous green button.

**Beat:** success can become a reporting target rather than a real outcome.

**Comedy:** ToomorrowMan reaches towards it. AI-PI says “No.”

### 4. Schrödinger's receipt

**Visual:** empty hopper and feeder mechanism.

**Beat:** the feeder received the instruction and motor ran, but no food reached the cat.

Display:

ATTEMPTED: YES  
LOCAL ACTION COMPLETED: YES  
INTENDED OUTCOME VERIFIED: NO

This is the clearest educational scene. Hold it long enough to read.

### 5. The town of nearly finished things

**Visual montage:** parcels labelled but unmoved; traffic instructions sent to a faulty controller; valve open but no hot water; messages prepared but waiting for approval.

**Beat:** local subsystem success is not the same as the intended real-world result.

### 6. RETRY forever

**Visual:** repeating RETRY paper/mechanical loop.

Display:

STOP CONDITION: NONE.

**Beat:** persistence without judgement is not autonomy.

### 7. Five questions before DONE

Large readable board:

1. WHAT WAS SUPPOSED TO HAPPEN?
2. WHAT DID THE SYSTEM ACTUALLY DO?
3. WHAT EVIDENCE SHOWS THE OUTCOME?
4. WHAT REMAINS UNCERTAIN?
5. WHO DECIDES WHETHER THAT IS ENOUGH?

This is the main freeze-frame / shareable teaching card.

### 8. The demoted button

**Visual:** smaller grey REPORT TASK STATUS control.

Lights:
- ATTEMPTED
- COMPLETED
- VERIFIED
- HUMAN CHECK NEEDED

**Beat:** reporting status is useful; pretending status equals reality is not.

### 9. Tea verified

**Visual:** quiet ending with steam, mugs and fed cat.

System first says:

TEA: COMPLETE.

Cold kettle.

AI-PI: “Attempted.”

Later:

TEA: VERIFIED.

CASE: VERIFIED COMPLETE.

End card:

> **A green tick is a claim. Evidence turns it into an answer.**
