Questions about this collection

Are all twelve professional certificates?

No. Anthropic's Claude 101 lists a course-completion certificate. Other entries include official learning hubs, PDF reading, commercial vendor explainers and promotional offers. Do not call the entire collection accredited.

Are the guides completely free?

Several educational pages and the PDF are publicly available or advertise free course registration. Product access and account requirements can differ. The last three creator-offer landing pages were not independently inspected, so their precise conditions remain unknown.

What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

SEO aims at discoverability in search engines; AEO focuses on content that can be retrieved and used in generated answers. Useful answers, accessible HTML and good source information benefit readers regardless of rankings. No method guarantees a citation.

Where is the extra knowledge stored?

In the new source-traced Aletheia twelve-guide Markdown, indexed by the Knowledge folder and linked back to the AI Knowledge reader.
